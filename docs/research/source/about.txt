# Who We Are
Fixcar.in is a doorstep vehicle service platform in Dehradun that makes car care easy, reliable, and hassle-free. It is first female owned Automobile startup of Uttarakhand.
Whether it’s regular maintenance, car washing, or emergency repairs, Fixcar.in brings expert mechanics and quality service right to your location—saving you time and effort. With a focus on convenience, transparency, and trust, the brand ensures that every customer gets professional-grade care without the need to visit a workshop. Based in Dehradun, Fixcar.in is redefining automobile servicing by combining technology with skilled expertise, offering a modern solution for today’s busy car owners.
