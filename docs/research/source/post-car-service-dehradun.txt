Your car works hard every day—whether you're commuting through Dehradun's busy roads, heading into the hills of Mussoorie, or taking a long highway trip across Uttarakhand. Like any machine, it needs regular maintenance to perform safely and efficiently.
Many car owners ignore small warning signs until a minor issue becomes a costly repair. Routine servicing not only improves your car's performance but also extends its lifespan, improves fuel efficiency, and reduces the risk of unexpected breakdowns.
In this guide, our automotive experts at Fixcar.in explain the 7 most common signs that your car needs servicing , why you should never ignore them, and how timely maintenance can save you thousands of rupees.

# 1. Unusual Engine Noises or Poor Performance
The engine is the heart of your vehicle. If it starts making unusual noises or feels less responsive, it should be inspected immediately.
Common warning signs include:
-
Knocking or ticking sounds from the engine
-
Difficulty starting the car
-
Poor acceleration
-
Engine overheating
-
Excessive smoke from the exhaust
-
Increased fuel consumption
Ignoring engine problems can lead to severe internal damage and expensive repairs.
A professional car service includes checking engine oil, filters, spark plugs, belts, cooling systems, and electronic diagnostics to identify problems before they become serious.
Expert Tip: If your Check Engine Light starts flashing instead of remaining steady, stop driving immediately and have your vehicle inspected.
# 2. Suspension Problems and an Uncomfortable Ride
Your suspension system keeps your vehicle stable, absorbs road shocks, and ensures a comfortable driving experience.
If your car feels unstable or uncomfortable while driving, your suspension may require attention.
Signs of suspension problems include:
-
Excessive bouncing after speed breakers
-
Steering wheel vibration
-
Uneven tyre wear
-
Car pulling to one side
-
Knocking sounds over rough roads
-
Poor handling while turning
Driving with worn suspension components reduces vehicle stability and increases tyre wear, making it a serious safety concern.
Routine servicing helps identify worn shock absorbers, suspension bushes, ball joints, and steering components before they fail.
# 3. Dashboard Warning Lights Are Illuminated
Modern vehicles continuously monitor important systems using sensors.
When a warning light appears, your car is telling you something needs attention.
Never ignore:
-
Check Engine Light
-
Battery Warning Light
-
Oil Pressure Warning
-
Brake Warning Light
-
ABS Warning Light
-
Coolant Temperature Warning
Many dashboard warnings begin as small issues but can quickly become expensive repairs if ignored.
Professional diagnostic scanning during servicing accurately identifies the problem and prevents unnecessary part replacements.
# 4. Poor Fuel Economy or Reduced Mileage
Has your car suddenly started consuming more fuel than usual?
Poor mileage is often one of the earliest signs that your vehicle needs servicing.
Possible reasons include:
-
Dirty air filter
-
Worn spark plugs
-
Old engine oil
-
Low tyre pressure
-
Fuel injector deposits
-
Engine tuning issues
A complete service restores engine efficiency and improves overall fuel economy.
If your monthly fuel expenses have noticeably increased, it's time for a professional inspection.
# 5. Brake Problems While Driving
Your braking system is one of the most critical safety components in your vehicle.
If you notice any changes in braking performance, don't delay servicing.
Warning signs include:
-
Squeaking or grinding brake noise
-
Soft or spongy brake pedal
-
Vehicle pulling during braking
-
Longer stopping distance
-
Steering vibration while braking
Brake pads, brake discs, brake fluid, and calipers should be inspected regularly to ensure maximum safety.
Driving with worn brakes significantly increases the risk of accidents.
# 6. Your Car Needs Professional Cleaning
Many people think washing a car is only about appearance.
In reality, regular cleaning protects your vehicle and helps preserve its value.
Professional cleaning helps:
-
Remove harmful dirt and contaminants
-
Protect your car's paint
-
Prevent rust formation
-
Improve interior hygiene
-
Remove bacteria, dust, and allergens
-
Maintain resale value
Interior dry cleaning also protects seats, carpets, dashboards, and air conditioning vents from long-term damage.
# 7. It's Been More Than Six Months Since Your Last Service
Sometimes your car doesn't show obvious warning signs.
However, important components naturally wear over time.
Most manufacturers recommend servicing every:
-
10,000 kilometres
OR
-
Every 6 months (whichever comes first)
If your vehicle frequently drives in city traffic, dusty roads, or hilly regions like Uttarakhand, more frequent servicing may be necessary.
Routine servicing usually includes:
✅ Engine inspection
✅ Brake inspection
✅ Suspension check
✅ Engine oil replacement
✅ Oil filter replacement
✅ Air filter cleaning
✅ Coolant inspection
✅ Battery testing
✅ Fluid top-up
✅ General vehicle health check
Preventive maintenance always costs less than major repairs.

Although every vehicle is different, these general guidelines apply:

Following your manufacturer's service schedule helps improve reliability and reduces long-term maintenance costs.

# Frequently Asked Questions
## How often should I service my car?
Most manufacturers recommend servicing every 10,000 km or every 6 months , whichever comes first. Vehicles driven in heavy traffic or hilly regions may require more frequent servicing.
## Can I drive with the Check Engine Light on?
A steady Check Engine Light may indicate a minor issue, but a flashing light usually signals a serious problem. Have your vehicle inspected immediately.
## Why is my car giving poor mileage?
Reduced fuel efficiency can be caused by dirty air filters, old engine oil, worn spark plugs, low tyre pressure, or overdue maintenance.
## Does regular servicing increase engine life?
Yes. Routine maintenance reduces wear, keeps components properly lubricated, improves cooling, and helps identify problems before they become major repairs.
